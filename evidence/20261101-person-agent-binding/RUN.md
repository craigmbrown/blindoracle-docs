## 0. What was being tested

v1 (2026-10-31) proved a Coinbase-verified person stood behind four agents, but nothing bound a *named person* to a *named agent* with a scope. v2 adds the person-signed grant (BO #326): the person wallet signs `{agent, credential_fp, allow_skus, max_usd_per_day, not_before/after, nonce}`; BO checks it when a board job is posted and again before release; the public receipt names the grant; `scripts/bo_grant_verify.py <job_id>` lets an outsider re-check signature (ERC-1271 on Base), Coinbase EAS attestation and scope.
